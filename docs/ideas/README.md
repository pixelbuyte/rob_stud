we are most likely to make. To start one, the owner names it as active in `/CLAUDE.md`, and it gets
a `games/<name>/GAME.md`.

## Money ideas for the co-op games

Both are co-op, so edges that help the **whole crew** are allowed if they pass the edge test in
`docs/MONETIZATION.md` (visible, limited, free path, skill still matters, a free crew can still
finish).

### TIDELIFT

| Product | Effect | Tier | Price (R$) | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crane paint sets, hook styles | Cosmetic, seen by whole crew | 0 | 75–199 | 1 |
| Ferry horns, landing fanfares | Plays on every delivery | 0–1 | 49–99 | 1 |
| Rescue bot skins (collection log) | Collect and display rescued bot looks | 0 | 49–149 | 2 |
| **Tugboat Delay** | Holds the next flood stage 20 s for the whole crew, once per run, shown to all | 3 | 25–49 | 2 |
| **Turbo Winch** pass | Crane moves 15% faster when the owner operates; whole crew benefits | 3 | 149 | 2 |
| Private dock (private server) | Play with your crew | 2 | 100 / month | 1 |

### CART PANIC

| Product | Effect | Tier | Price (R$) | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cart skins, horns, wheel trails | Cosmetic | 0 | 75–199 | 1 |
| Victory checkout animations | Plays on extraction | 0–1 | 99 | 1 |
| **Bulk Basket** | +2 cart slots for one run for the whole team, shown on the cart | 3 | 25–49 | 2 |
| **Robot Whistle** | Freezes the cleaning robot 8 s once per run | 3 | 25 | 2 |
| Private store (private server) | Play with your crew | 2 | 100 / month | 1 |

Score in both games stays in-game points, never Robux. No paid loot boxes.
