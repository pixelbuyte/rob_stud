Recorded as the owner pasted them. Claude's review of these is in
`docs/ideas/wrong-answer-only/suggestions.md` on branch `rob-clud_1`.

> I'd change three things before the first playtest.

| # | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Give the button room a clue.** One correct button out of 20 makes survival a blind guess, especially in a two-player match. Reveal one clue every 10 seconds, such as highlighting the correct row, so players have to decide whether to risk an early press or wait. |
| 2 | **Use a short sudden-death round if multiple players survive Round 5.** Send them back to the two-zone arena, show each safe-zone cue, and shorten the grace window with each swap. That resolves a match the guide currently leaves open. |
| 3 | **Playtest with four friends before adding revives.** A two-player match can end at the first trap, so four players gives you a better look at the later rounds and spectator mode. For paid items, start with private servers and funny elimination effects. A revive changes who gets to stay in the competition. |
